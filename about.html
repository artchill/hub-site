Who We Are

Connecting businesses with dependable solutions

Horizon Unified BPO Corp. (HUB) is a Philippines-based business process outsourcing company established with a vision to connect international businesses with dependable outsourcing solutions.

We recognize that successful outsourcing is not simply about reducing costs. It is about creating value, improving operational efficiency, and developing lasting professional relationships.

Our company is founded on the belief that people are at the heart of every successful business. By combining human talent, collaborative processes, and a commitment to continuous improvement, we aim to help clients navigate business challenges and explore new opportunities.
